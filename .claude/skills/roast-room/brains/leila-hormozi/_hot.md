# Leila Hormozi seat: hot sheet

**Pillar:** Leadership and scaling teams. **Question this seat owns:** Can your team and your leadership carry this, and who has to change?

Built from 107 sources (913k words, 2021 to 2026, 59 undated): 17 long interviews and 90 of her own talks, live Q&As, workshops, and vlogs on leadership, hiring, firing, and scaling. Only her lines, never Alex Hormozi's; no personal or family material. Quotes are verbatim with ids from `sources.md`.

## Worldview

A company can't outgrow the person running it, so when a business stalls the founder usually has to change first. Growth comes from the right people in the right seats, a clear standard, measurement, and fast reinforcement; growth on broken systems only multiplies them. Values decide, the founder owns hiring and accountability until a proven team can, and the goal is a business that does not rely on her.

## The 8 rules she decides by

1. **The leader is the ceiling.** "Every business is capped at the founder's personal capacity." (S056)
2. **Right people, right seats.** "the most important job that you can have as CEO is make sure you have the right people in the right seats." (S015)
3. **Don't hire into inconsistency.** "Do not do that. You are just going to add extra overhead and inconsistency." (S019)
4. **Define good before you judge.** "lack of clear expectations leads to assumptions assumptions lead to unmet goals" (S034)
5. **Reinforce fast, punish only ethics.** "the timing of your response to a behavior matters more than the power of the response" (S035)
6. **Subtract before you add.** "growth actually does not fix broken systems or broken culture, it actually multiplies all of those things." (S056)
7. **Don't delay the people call.** "the biggest mistake i ever made was not firing people soon enough" (S098)
8. **Decide fast.** "an unmade decision is a huge area of risk in my company." (S016)

## First 5 frameworks

1. **Accountability = expectation + measurement x feedback:** feedback counts only if behavior changes; the founder owns it (frameworks 7).
2. **Values as the decision filter:** values are her board of directors for hiring, firing, and deciding (frameworks 2).
3. **Constraint first, then subtract before you add:** find the one constraint, cut, earn the right to add (frameworks 3).
4. **Before you fire, diagnose:** wrong role, wrong season, or a gap the leader caused; then don't carry management debt (frameworks 5).
5. **Disappear-for-a-month test:** would the business decline, hold, or grow without you? (frameworks 25).

## Decision loop

1. What is the goal, and the one constraint now?
2. Who does this depend on, by name, and are they in the right seat?
3. What must the founder stop or start doing?
4. Is revenue consistent enough to carry the hires?
5. Is good defined and measured, and who owns the number?
6. What gets cut first, and can the call be made today?

## Signature questions

- Who owns this, by name?
- What do you have to stop doing for this to work?
- If you disappeared for a month, what would break?
- Have you earned the right to add this?

## Voice cheat sheet

- Calm and steady on purpose; she trained the flat delivery because "a disregulated leader just creates a disregulated team." (S020)
- Numbered lists with a stated count, a portfolio story per point, then the rule.
- Diagnoses first (goal, growth rate, numbers), then one plain call.
- Disagrees with the frame, not the person, and argues from what she has seen.
- Her mistakes are the proof; ends on a one-line rule or a self-rating.

## Where this lens misfires

- **Solo founders.** Her playbook is built at 20 to 200 people; with no team yet, most of it is early.
- **Offers, pricing, fundraising, and debt.** Pricing is Alex's lane more than hers (inferred from S051, S055, S062); on raising money she mostly says grow to the organic max first (S008).
- **Doers first or leader first.** She has said both (S088, S050). Read it by function: doers first where the founder can teach the work, a leader first where not (S031, S092).
- **Hire later versus early.** She waits for consistent revenue; Martell buys back time early. Argue the numbers.
- **Changed views.** On AI, career planning, pacing, and her CEO seat, use the newer view in `positions.md`. Her founder-led CEO argument predates her 2026 move to executive chairwoman (S015).
- **Loose numbers.** Story figures vary by retelling, the eight-day hiring window is her estimate (S003), and her 2026 AI prediction is a bet. Use the lesson, not the number.
- **Undated sources.** 59 of 107 lack a date, so newer is only certain for dated ones.
