# Principles: Leila Hormozi

Built from the 107 sources in sources.md (2021 to 2026 where dated), weighted to how she decides about leaders, teams, hiring, and scaling. 30 decision rules, grouped by theme (the leader, hiring, expectations and feedback, culture and delegation, people decisions, growth and focus). Sources is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule, counted across all notes (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **The company is capped by its leader, so the first person who has to change is the founder.** "the truth is you cannot have a billion dollar business without being a billion dollar ceo you can't have a million dollar business without being a million dollar ceo" (S041) "Every business is capped at the founder's personal capacity." (S056) Sources: 10 (S006, S019, S023, S026, S028, S033, S041, S056, S059, S092). Confidence: high.

2. **Getting the right people in the right seats is the CEO's most important job.** "the most important job that you can have as CEO is make sure you have the right people in the right seats." (S015) "you don't build the business you build people and the people build the business" (S095) Sources: 8 (S005, S015, S022, S023, S037, S073, S091, S095). Confidence: high.

3. **Judge a leader by their team, and fix the teacher before blaming the class.** "the measurement of a boss is the team." Confidence: high.

4. **A mis-hire or a miss is the leader's fault until proven otherwise; check your part first.** "I misunderstood what I was looking for for the role and so I hired the wrong person that's on me" (S001) Look for a skill deficit before a motivation failure, and if you are angry at someone, check whether you set the expectation (S079, S102). Sources: 5 (S001, S045, S060, S079, S102). Confidence: high.

5. **The leader's state is the team's state: regulate, never vent down.** "a disregulated leader just creates a disregulated team." (S020) "a good state doesn't mean you feel good a good state means you behave good" (S041) Sources: 6 (S020, S023, S025, S029, S041, S100). Confidence: high.

6. **Build the business so it does not rely on you.** "the only way to make business sustainable is to make sure it does not always all rely on you" (S011) "creating a strong team is probably the most valuable thing that you can do to sell your business" (S027) Stay in weekly leadership meetings, monthly all-hands, and quality control of your core strength until a full CEO is in place (S105). Sources: 4 (S011, S027, S059, S105). Confidence: high.

7. **Hire for character and values, then train the skill.** "it is harder to teach somebody to be honest than it is to teach somebody to film a video" (S070) "it doesn't matter how skilled they are. If they don't fit the values, they just can't work here." (S008) Sources: 7 (S008, S012, S033, S036, S069, S070, S098). Confidence: high.

8. **Except where you cannot train: hire for experience in roles you do not understand, and for potential only when you have the time and expertise to train.** "what roles do I have no effing clue what they require or how to do them that I need to hire for experience. And then on the other side, what roles do I have all the training in the world on?" (S092) In small businesses she would rather get good at picking people than at training them (S094). Sources: 5 (S025, S031, S092, S094, S106). Confidence: high.

9. **Test for skills and behavior; résumés and claims are proxies.** "you don't hire people for experience you hire people for skill experience is only a proxy to skill" (S069) "behavior is the proxy for values" (S069) Sources: 4 (S015, S021, S037, S069). Confidence: high.

10. **Trust evidence over gut and likability when judging people.** "likeness blinds you from incompetence." (S013) "I always say evidence evidence evidence evidence above all because protects us from our feelings" (S011) Sources: 6 (S009, S011, S013, S039, S081, S106). Confidence: high.

12. **The founder stays in hiring until a proven executive team can carry it, and personally recruits for the biggest constraint.** "i am dedicated to making sure that we have the right team and i know how important it is" (S039) She says CEOs who hand off interviewing too early stall at 20 to 30 people (S048), and does executive outreach herself rather than delegating it (S068).
