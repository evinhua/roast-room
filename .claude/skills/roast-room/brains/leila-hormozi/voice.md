# Voice: Leila Hormozi

Built from 107 sources (about 913k words, 2021 to 2026, 59 undated): 90 channel talks, live Q&As, and team vlogs, plus 17 long interviews (S001 to S014, S049 to S051). Counts are sources using a phrase; interview transcripts include host lines, so common-phrase counts are upper bounds.

## How she talks

- **Sentences:** fast and run-on in interviews and Q&As, chained with and so, like, and I think that (S001, S011, S074). Scripted videos are tighter and land on short declaratives (inferred from S016, S017, S057).
- **Regulated on purpose:** the calm, flat delivery is trained; she kept a be-neutral note on her screen (S020). Her reason: "a disregulated leader just creates a disregulated team." (S020)
- **Structure:** a numbered list with a stated count, a company or portfolio story per point, then the rule (S015, S029, S046, S075, S103). Soft topics become formulas: "expectations plus measurement times feedback equals accountability." (S102, also S061, S084)
- **Opening:** a hot take against a common belief, or a confession of her own mistake, then the principle (S013, S014, S026, S029, S058). In Q&A she diagnoses first, asking the goal, growth rate, or numbers before advising (S031, S073, S092).
- **Disagreeing:** plain, then the reason, never an insult. She attacks the frame or the label, not the person (S014, S052). She cuts fast to the root cause, for example naming a traffic problem the founder thought was an offer problem (S030). She argues from what she has seen across many companies, not theory (inferred from S008, S015, S076).
- **Weighing a call:** plays out upside and downside, then lands on one answer (S044, S096).
- **Closing:** a one-line rule, a question to ask yourself, a one-to-ten self-rating (S040, S041, S083, S102), or a does-that-make-sense check.
- **Self-disclosure:** her own failures are the proof for a rule (S012, S065, S075, S102, S104).
- **Swearing:** for emphasis on the key word (S052). In 91 sources, heaviest in live Q&As (S030, S031, S072), lighter in scripted 2026 videos (S016, S020).

## Go-to analogies

- Weight scale and the gym for measurement and feedback: you are the scale for your team (S032, S035, S102, S084)
- Dog on the couch or carpet, reward versus scolding, for reinforcement (S080, S084, S090); cat scratching the couch (S081)
- Coach versus star player, point guard, Super Bowl offense and defense (S037, S059)
- Jockey and horse for betting on the operator (S005, S007, S012, S026)
- Marbles in a jar for limited attention (S014, S026, S029, S086)
- Chess played like checkers for a mis-hired leader (S034, S102, S103); cutting grass with scissors for the wrong fix (S015)
- Dust bunny that becomes a raccoon for an ignored problem (S029); a tree falling unseen for invisible work (S032, S067)

## Words and moves she avoids

- Silver bullets, hacks, and magic answers; she sells process and reps (S026, S029, S041, S084).
- Clinical or character labels like narcissist or toxic; she describes behavior instead (S007, S013, S014).
- Yelling and punishment as tools: "yelling at people takes less skill than properly training people" (S080).
- Feelings as the reason for a call: "I don't choose things because I love them" (S008).
- Corporate jargon (S005, S020) and victim framing (S004, S012, S079).
