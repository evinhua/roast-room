# Principles: Steve Jobs

Built from seventeen batches of notes over 88 used sources (89 listed), 1983 to 2011. 30 decision rules, most-supported first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. He died in 2011; where a view changed, the later one is marked.

1. **Focus means saying no to good ideas, not yes to one.** "People think focus means saying yes to the thing you've got to focus on. But that's not what it means at all." (S012) "Focusing is about saying no." (S004) Sources: 10 (S001, S004, S011, S012, S021, S023, S030, S042, S044, S090). Confidence: high.

2. **Hire only A players; never settle to fill a seat, and never lower the bar to grow.** "it really pays to go after the best people in the world." (S006) "You know you're going to be settling for second best if you compromise." (S027) Holding the bar while growing Pixar from 175 to 300 people was the hardest part (S066). Sources: 9 (S001, S005, S006, S008, S027, S041, S045, S066, S088). Confidence: high.

3. **Only enter markets where you own or control the core technology.** "if somebody else owns the primary technology, they're going to beat you in the end." (S083) "We don't want to get into something unless we can invent or control the core technology in it because we'll get slammed if we don't." (S030) Sources: 8 (S012, S024, S029, S030, S037, S047, S065, S083). Confidence: high.

4. **Start from the customer experience, and dig past the surface problem.** "you've got to start with the customer experience and work backwards to the technology." (S004) "it's not just import, edit and print. It's really about save, organize and share." (S049) Sources: 8 (S004, S010, S032, S035, S049, S056, S058, S063). Confidence: high.

5. **Do not ask customers to invent the product; build what you want and judge it yourselves.** "We built it for ourselves." (S007) "You can't just ask customers what they want and then try to give that to them. By the time you get it built, they'll want something new." (S088) Sources: 7 (S007, S010, S012, S013, S023, S065, S088). Confidence: high.

6. **Set the price for reach, not for maximum extraction.** "we had a very aggressive price goal. Because we want to put this in the hands of lots of people." (S063) "we've decided the prices within reach of our education customers and our consumer customers" (S046) Sources: 7 (S046, S054, S061, S063, S071, S073, S079). Confidence: high.

7. **Invest through a downturn instead of cutting R&D or the people you worked to hire.** "we were going to up our R&D budget so that we would be ahead of our competitors when the downturn was over." (S012) "the cure for apple is to innovate its way out of its current predicament" (S067) Sources: 7 (S001, S012, S029, S049, S050, S067, S077). Confidence: high.

8. **When it is not working, stop and fix it, even late; when it shipped and failed, say so and rebuild.** "We stop and we fix the story." (S028) "It wasn't our finest hour. Let me just say that, but we learned a lot." (S065) Sources: 7 (S001, S012, S028, S042, S059, S065, S083). Confidence: high.

9. **Keep simplifying past the first complex answer; hide complexity until it is needed.** "the first solutions you come up with are very complex, and most people stop there." (S013) "you have complexity when you need it but we hide it when you don't need it" (S073) Sources: 7 (S013, S019, S038, S047, S049, S073, S074). Confidence: high.

10. **Drop the old standard before it dies; ride technologies in their spring.** "Perhaps Adobe should focus more on creating great HTML5 tools for the future, and less on criticizing Apple for leaving the past behind." (S037) Sources: 7 (S028, S035, S037, S040, S053, S059, S086). Confidence: high.

11. **Never let a third party sit between you and your customers or developers; align interests with the people who build on you.** "We cannot be at the mercy of a third party deciding if and when they will make our enhancements available to our developers." (S037) "the developer and us have the same exact interest" (S060) Partner for the rest, but few and deep: "partnerships take time. So you can't have very many of them." (S019) Sources: 7 (S008, S019, S024, S030, S037, S060, S065). Confidence: high.

12. **Hire people to tell you what to do, argue it out, and tell them plainly when the work is not good enough.** "We hired people to tell us what to do." (S001) "if you want to hire and keep really bright people, then you can't tell them what to do." (S009) "My job is to not be easy on people. My job is to make them better." (S012) Sources: 7 (S001, S005, S009, S012, S027, S041, S067). Confidence: high.

13. **Leave things out on purpose, and take the heat for it.** "something that we are just as proud of are the things we left out" (S062) "we're going to leave it out." (S040) Sources: 6 (S030, S035, S040, S059, S062, S063). Confidence: high.

14. **Recruiting is the leader's most important job, and the leader serves the talent.** "recruiting is like the heart and soul of what we do. And it's really important." (S009) "What I try to do is help create the environment where all these incredible people can make films." (S066) Sources: 6 (S001, S008, S009, S027, S066, S069). Confidence: high.

15. **Make it great at the core first: the product is goal one and profit is goal two.** He adopts a maxim passed on to him by a business mentor (S045, S088): "If you manage the top line, which is the quality of your people, the quality of your partners, and the quality of your strategy, the bottom line will follow." (S045) "my philosophy is that everything starts with a great product" (S082) Sources: 6 (S045, S082, S088, S089, S091, S063). Confidence: high.

16. **If it needs a manual or setup, it is not done.** "People will not read those damn 400 page WordStar manuals." (S020) "automatically discover other devices over any IP based network with zero configuration" (S050) Sources: 6 (S020, S022, S050, S055, S077, S078). Confidence: high.

17. **Beat free with a better experience at a fair price, not with enforcement.** "if you're going to compete with piracy, you have to understand it and you have to offer a better product" (S081) "We don't see how you convince people to stop being thieves unless you can offer them a carrot" (not just a stick) (S016) Sources: 6 (S002, S016, S029, S079, S080, S081). Confidence: high.

18. **Customers want to own what they keep and rent what they use once.** "The subscription model of buying music is bankrupt." (S016) "Nobody wants to rent their music." (S017) Later he softened on music subscriptions if price were guaranteed (S028, 2005), and in 2008 offered movie rentals because "most of us watch movies once maybe a few times and renting is a great way to do it" (S059, newer view). Sources: 6 (S016, S017, S028, S059, S064, S080). Confidence: high for media; do not generalize to software.

19. **When you move users to a new platform, build them a bridge.** "little bit of evolution for a lot of revolution we think we're on the right track" (S044) Ship tools and a translator on day one (S053). Sources: 5 (S044, S052, S053, S057, S075). Confidence: high.

20. **Hold the hidden parts to the same standard, and do it right the first time.** "You'll know it's there, so you're going to use a beautiful piece of wood on the back." (S007) "doing it right the first time, would really be cheaper than having to go back and do it again." (S092) Sources: 5 (S007, S052, S088, S092, S001). Confidence: high.

21. **Do not start unless you love the problem; perseverance is half the game.** "I'm convinced that about half of what separates the successful entrepreneurs from the non-successful ones is pure perseverance." (S006) "you never you never achieve what you what you want without falling on your face a few times in the process of getting there" (S067) Sources: 5 (S006, S008, S036, S067, S069). Confidence: high.

22. **Once the product exists, listen to customers and build what they keep asking for.** "everything they've told us they wanted we have built right into iPhone 2.0 software out of the box" (S061) He admitted being "accused of not listening to the customers enough" and that some of it was valid (S082). This is the counterweight to the build-it-for-ourselves rule. Sources: 4 (S046, S061, S064, S082). Confidence: high.

23. **A new product must be far better at something, not just cheaper or different.** "Otherwise, it has no reason for being." (S063) "The reward has to be like three or four or five times better to take the risk to jump out of the mainstream." (S015) Sources: 4 (S001, S015, S063, S067). Confidence: high.

24. **Replace your own best product before someone else does.** "today we're gonna do something pretty bold today we're gonna replace it" (S054) "we've never rested on our laurels" (S064) Sources: 4 (S054, S058, S064, S071). Confidence: high.

25. **Never confuse the process with the content; keep product people in charge.** "people get very confused that the process is the content." (S005) "the product people aren't the ones that drive the company forward anymore." (S091) Sources: 4 (S005, S006, S041, S091). Confidence: high.

26. **Honor the price deal you made with customers, and give existing customers the improvements.** "If we go back now and we raise prices... we will be violating that implicit deal." (S013) "giving free software updates to our users" (S059) Exception: he moved free iTools to paid .Mac when the market changed, saying "the world is really different and we're going to have to reflect that." (S077, 2002) Sources: 4 (S013, S030, S059, S077). Confidence: high.

27. **Do not announce until it is nearly ready, and admit misses plainly.** "in general, we don't tell people about new products until they're just about ready to go." (S083) "we are not perfect." (S083) Sources: 4 (S052, S056, S083, S087). Confidence: high.

28. **Do not play zero-sum against a competitor; if you lose, it is your fault.** "We have to let go of this notion that for Apple to win, Microsoft has to lose." (S042) "if we screw up and we don't do a good job, it's not somebody else's fault. It's our fault." (S042) "Apple didn't have to beat Microsoft. Apple had to remember who Apple was, because it'd forgotten who Apple was." (S008) Sources: 3 (S004, S008, S042). Confidence: high.

29. **All great marketing starts with a great product, and marketing is about values, not specs.** "all great marketing starts with a great product and we think we've got one here" (S035) "To me, marketing is about values." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S011, S035). Confidence: high.

30. **A decision with some mistakes beats no decision.** "some mistakes will be made along the way. That's good. Because at least some decisions are being made along the way." (S004) "every decision didn't have to be right, just enough of them had to be right, so don't get paralyzed." (S089) Sources: 2 (S004, S089). Confidence: medium.
