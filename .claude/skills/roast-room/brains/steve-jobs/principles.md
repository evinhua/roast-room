# Principles: Steve Jobs

Built from six batches of notes over 38 sources, 1983 to 2010. 28 decision rules. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. He died in 2011; where a view changed, the later one is marked.

1. **Focus means saying no to good ideas, not yes to one.** "People think focus means saying yes to the thing you've got to focus on. But that's not what it means at all." (S012) "Focusing is about saying no." (S004) Sources: 7 (S001, S004, S011, S012, S021, S023, S030). Confidence: high.

2. **Cut the product line to what your best people can make great.** "if we only had four, we could put the A team on every single one of them." (S001) Sources: 4 (S001, S011, S021, S023). Confidence: high.

3. **Hire only A players; never settle to fill a seat.** "it really pays to go after the best people in the world." (S006) "You know you're going to be settling for second best if you compromise." (S027) Sources: 6 (S001, S005, S006, S027, S041, S008). Confidence: high.

4. **Recruiting is the leader's most important job.** "recruiting is like the heart and soul of what we do. And it's really important." (S009) "I spend 20 percent of my time recruiting, even now." (S001) Sources: 4 (S001, S008, S009, S027). Confidence: high.

5. **Hire people to tell you what to do, then get out of their way.** "We hired people to tell us what to do." (S001) "if you want to hire and keep really bright people, then you can't tell them what to do." (S009) Sources: 3 (S001, S009, S041). Confidence: high.

6. **Do not ask customers to invent the product; build what you want and judge it yourselves.** "We built it for ourselves." (S007) "We do no market research. We don't hire consultants." (S012) Sources: 5 (S007, S010, S012, S013, S023). Confidence: high.

7. **Start from the customer experience, never from the technology.** "you've got to start with the customer experience and work backwards to the technology." (S004) Sources: 4 (S004, S010, S032, S035; S010, S032, S035 inferred). Confidence: high.

8. **Making it great costs time and care, not much more money.** "All it takes is a little more time." (S001) "it doesn't cost any more money to make them look great." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S007, S041). Confidence: high.

9. **Hold the hidden parts to the same standard as the visible ones.** "You'll know it's there, so you're going to use a beautiful piece of wood on the back." (S007) Sources: 1 (S007), applied in S019 and S027 to circuit boards and the factory (inferred). Confidence: low on direct statement, medium in practice.

10. **Keep simplifying past the first complex answer.** "the first solutions you come up with are very complex, and most people stop there." (S013) Sources: 3 (S013, S019, S038). Confidence: high.

11. **When it is not working, stop and fix it, even late and even when it is expensive.** "We stop and we fix the story." (S028) "I just don't love this. I can't convince myself to fall in love with this." (S012) Sources: 3 (S001, S012, S028). Confidence: high.

12. **Only enter markets where you own or control the core technology.** "We don't want to get into something unless we can invent or control the core technology in it because we'll get slammed if we don't." (S030) Sources: 5 (S012, S024, S029, S030, S037). Confidence: high.

13. **Never let a third party sit between you and your customers or developers.** "We cannot be at the mercy of a third party deciding if and when they will make our enhancements available to our developers." (S037) Sources: 3 (S024, S030, S037). Confidence: high.

14. **Partner for what you do not do, and keep the partnerships few and deep.** "We don't think one company can do everything." (S008) "partnerships take time. So you can't have very many of them." (S019) Sources: 3 (S008, S018, S019). Confidence: high.

15. **If you ask people to leave the mainstream, be several times better.** "The reward has to be like three or four or five times better to take the risk to jump out of the mainstream." (S015) Sources: 2 (S001, S015). Confidence: medium.

16. **Have the courage to leave things out, and take the heat for it.** "we're going to leave it out." (S040) Sources: 3 (S030, S035, S040). Confidence: high.

17. **Drop the old standard before it dies; ride technologies in their spring.** "Perhaps Adobe should focus more on creating great HTML5 tools for the future, and less on criticizing Apple for leaving the past behind." (S037) Sources: 4 (S028, S035, S037, S040). Confidence: high.

18. **Do not bloat: companies die of indigestion more than starvation.** "more companies tend to die of indigestion than starvation." (S019) Sources: 2 (S019, S026). Confidence: medium.

19. **All great marketing starts with a great product, and marketing is about values, not specs.** "all great marketing starts with a great product and we think we've got one here" (S035) "To me, marketing is about values." (S001) Sources: 3 (S001, S011, S035). Confidence: high.

20. **Beat free with a better experience at a fair price, not with enforcement.** "We don't see how you convince people to stop being thieves unless you can offer them a carrot" (not just a stick) (S016) Sources: 4 (S002, S016, S028, S029). Confidence: high.

21. **Honor the price deal you made with customers.** "If we go back now and we raise prices... we will be violating that implicit deal." (S013) Sources: 2 (S013, S030). Confidence: medium.

22. **Customers want to own, not rent (his music-era view, 2003 to 2005).** "The subscription model of buying music is bankrupt." (S016) "Nobody wants to rent their music." (S017) Later he softened: he would consider a subscription if the price were guaranteed stable for years (S028, 2005, newer view). Sources: 3 (S016, S017, S028). Confidence: high for music; do not generalize to software.

23. **Invest through a downturn instead of cutting R&D or the people you worked to hire.** "we were going to up our R&D budget so that we would be ahead of our competitors when the downturn was over." (S012) Sources: 3 (S001, S012, S029). Confidence: high.

24. **Never confuse the process with the content; question every habit.** "people get very confused that the process is the content." (S005) "They're done because they were done yesterday and the day before." (S005) Sources: 2 (S005, S006). Confidence: medium.

25. **Tell good people plainly when their work is not good enough.** "My job is to not be easy on people. My job is to make them better." (S012) Sources: 3 (S005, S012, S027). Confidence: high.

26. **Do not play zero-sum against a competitor when you do not have to.** "Apple didn't have to beat Microsoft. Apple had to remember who Apple was, because it'd forgotten who Apple was." (S008) Sources: 2 (S004, S008). Confidence: medium.

27. **A decision with some mistakes beats no decision.** "some mistakes will be made along the way. That's good. Because at least some decisions are being made along the way." (S004) Sources: 1 (S004). Confidence: low.

28. **Do not start unless you love the problem; perseverance is half the game.** "I'm convinced that about half of what separates the successful entrepreneurs from the non-successful ones is pure perseverance." (S006) "if you don't any rational person would give up." (S008) Sources: 3 (S006, S008, S036). Confidence: high.
