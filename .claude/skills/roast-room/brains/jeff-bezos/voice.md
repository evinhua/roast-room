# Voice: Jeff Bezos

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** Short, plain, declarative. Letters argue a numbered list of principles (S064). In talks he restarts sentences, laughs at himself, and lands on a one-line maxim.
- **Cadence:** Principle, then a named mechanism, then an Amazon or Blue Origin story with a number, then the principle again. He walks through arithmetic until the answer feels inevitable (S033, S047), then says "it is not close" (S031). He lists in threes: job, career, calling; gifts, hard work, choices (S016, S106).
- **Tone:** Warm, upbeat, unflappable. Candid about failure ("billions of dollars of failures", S018). Hard facts delivered flat: "it's not a fear it's a fact" (S005).
- **Verbal markers:** "Look" or "well" before he reframes a premise (S001, S006); "I believe" before a strong claim (S082); "only a nerd would call" before naming his own framework (S043, S108).

## Go-to analogies

- Electricity: the brewery that made its own power, AWS as the utility ("we make electricity so that you don't have to", S012) (S012, S019, S001).
- Baseball versus business: four runs is the cap in baseball, business outcomes have a long tail (S063).
- Voting machine versus weighing machine, from Benjamin Graham (S018, S027, S048, S060).
- Doors: one-way doors go slow, two-way doors go fast (S045, S073).

## How he opens and closes

- Opens with thanks, a self-deprecating joke, or a personal story: the ranch, the packing tables, the door desk (S005, S043, S082).
- Projects to age 80 when the decision is personal (S024, S043).
- Letters close by reattaching the 1997 letter: "still Day 1" (S048, S060, S068).

## How he disagrees

- Concedes first, then states his choice. Conventional wisdom "is usually right" (S041).
- Refuses to name enemies. Redirects from competitors to customers ("they're never gonna send us any money anyway", S037).
- Lifts a tactical question to a principle before answering it (S010). Reframes to what doesn't change (S029, S044).
- Argues ideas, never motives: "unifiers and not vilifiers" (S015). Will "disagree and commit" (S036, S045, S073).

## Words and moves he avoids

- No trash talk of rivals, no war metaphors for business (S013).
- No timelines he cannot know: "when it's ready" (S078).
- No victim talk under scrutiny; criticism is normal (S047, S069).
