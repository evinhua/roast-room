# Principles: Sara Blakely

Built from 54 usable sources of 60 fetched (keynotes, long interviews, podcasts, live streams, a commencement address, and short web pieces, 2013 to 2026, about 331,000 words; see `sources.md`), merged from nine batches of verified notes covering 52 of them (S055 and S056 were read directly). 30 decision rules, most-used first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Lines she retells from other people (her father, Warren Buffett, George Foreman, buyers) are not quoted as hers.

1. **Fund the business from its own sales, start small, and keep the ownership.** "I only ever bought or spent what I had orders for already or what I knew I could sell" (S007) "I've never taken any additional outside funding in 20 years." (S014) Sources: 21 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S006, S007, S008, S010, S014, S015, S016, S019, S020, S021, S029, S032, S037, S040, S042, S045, S054). Confidence: high. She allows that some business models need capital (S045), and admits self-funding left her tired (S008).

2. **Not knowing how it is done is an edge, so act on it instead of waiting for credentials.** "what you don't know can be your greatest asset if you let it, if you have the courage." (S008) "when you don't know how something's supposed to be done, you're insured, you're going to do it differently. And that's where innovation is." (S045) Sources: 20 (S001, S002, S004, S007, S008, S009, S017, S018, S019, S020, S021, S022, S024, S029, S035, S041, S045, S053, S054, S058). Confidence: high.

3. **Start with a problem you personally have; you are the first customer.** "I was a frustrated consumer. I had never taken a business class. I had never worked in fashion or retail" (S035) "people become entrepreneurs for two reasons they invent something that didn't already exist and fill white space or they take something that existed and dramatically improve it" (S037) Sources: 18 (S006, S008, S009, S018, S021, S022, S023, S026, S029, S032, S033, S035, S037, S038, S039, S043, S059, S060). Confidence: high.

4. **Failure is not trying, not a bad outcome; go toward what scares you.** "failure was no longer for me the outcome failure was not trying" (S016) "I've kind of um looked at the things that I'm afraid of, like I'm afraid of being embarrassed, afraid of failure, all these different things, and I go straight towards them." (S033) Sources: 16 (S001, S005, S007, S012, S015, S016, S017, S019, S020, S024, S029, S030, S033, S037, S053, S054). Confidence: high.

5. **When the product is best proven live, go in person and show it.** "I knew from cold calling that my best shot was in person." (S001) "I could tell I was losing her. So I said, you know what, Diane? Will you come with me to the bathroom?" (S031) Sources: 14 (S001, S002, S006, S007, S013, S015, S016, S018, S022, S023, S031, S035, S040, S053). Confidence: high.

6. **Protect time alone to think, and write down every idea the moment it comes.** "You have to protect your time alone. You have to protect your time to think, to daydream, to visualize where you want to end up" (S024) "wherever it is in your life that your mind wanders is where the magic is for you" (S039) Sources: 15 (S001, S005, S006, S007, S008, S009, S015, S019, S024, S025, S026, S039, S040, S054, S058). Confidence: high.

7. **Anchor every venture in a why bigger than yourself; it supplies the courage.** "if your why is bigger than yourself like I care deeply about elevating women... that gives me courage that I probably wouldn't normally have" (S005) "if you're not clear on your why you will become a lot more volatile in I think other people's opinions and all the noise that comes on around you" (S005) Sources: 15 (S001, S004, S005, S007, S010, S011, S014, S032, S037, S041, S045, S050, S055, S057, S059). Confidence: high.

8. **Hire your weaknesses as soon as you can afford it, and spend your own day on your superpowers.** "as soon as you can afford to hire your weaknesses do it" (S041) "at the end of each day, a good litmus test is did I spend the majority of my dayto-day doing what I'm best at?" (S009) Sources: 12 (S007, S009, S015, S016, S018, S019, S023, S026, S029, S040, S041, S054). Confidence: high.

9. **Getting the order is the start; go sell it yourself until the floor sells it for you.** "I was building an entire salesforce that wasn't on my payroll." (S054) "I was absolutely determined to ensure my own success" (S016) Sources: 12 (S002, S004, S007, S010, S016, S018, S022, S031, S041, S042, S053, S054). Confidence: high.

10. **Let customers be the advertising; earn word of mouth before you pay for reach.** "Spanx didn't spend any money on advertising for 16 years." (S004) "Spanx didn't advertise for 16 years. We became a household name and a household brand around the world without ever advertising." (S053) Sources: 12 (S001, S004, S006, S007, S010, S011, S013, S015, S022, S039, S041, S053). Confidence: high. *How it changed:* no ads began as a budget limit and became a kept choice once she saw how authentic word of mouth felt (inferred from S004, S011, S022).

11. **Take input, then decide from your gut; intuition wins when it conflicts with data.** "I always choose in my whole journey I chose intuition over data." (S021) "you're never going to find intuition on a spreadsheet." (S009) Sources: 12 (S003, S004, S005, S009, S013, S015, S019, S021, S039, S040, S043, S045). Confidence: high. She offers no case where her gut misled her; the council should treat that as a blind spot.

12. **Go straight to the decision maker and keep filling the funnel with no's.** "you have to have a certain amount of no's to get to the yes... you don't want to stop filling the top of the funnel with no's." (S014) "I called them yeah I picked up the phone" (S006) Sources: 11 (S001, S002, S006, S007, S013, S014, S022, S029, S030, S032, S041). Confidence: high.

13. **Do not build a company around a product that is only a bit better; it must be 10x, and cut it when it is not.** "I really believe if you're going to start a company or start go into a new category, your product should be 10x better than what's already out there" (S021) "if you're going to create a product, you want to make sure it's exponentially better than what's already out there." (S009) Sources: 10 (S007, S009, S019, S021, S026, S037, S038, S041, S044, S045). Confidence: high.

14. **Fix the destination and leave the route open; be specific about what you want.** "I really I really feel like you should see it first" (S016) "I wrote down, I want to invent something that will change millions of people's lives for the better." (S004) Sources: 10 (S001, S002, S004, S008, S011, S014, S016, S026, S043, S053). Confidence: high.

15. **Vulnerability and humor sell better than polish; you do not need to act serious to be taken seriously.** "a lot of people think of vulnerability as weakness and I don't I think it's a big strength" (S030) "I don't subscribe to the fact that you have to act serious to be taken seriously." (S015) Sources: 10 (S001, S006, S007, S011, S015, S020, S029, S030, S037, S053). Confidence: high.

16. **Sell the problem and the why, not the features.** "i always say you know sell the problem you're solving not the product people are far more emotional about that" (S020) "lead with purpose. Lead with your story." (S050) Sources: 8 (S004, S007, S010, S016, S020, S041, S050, S053). Confidence: high.

17. **Product is king; marketing never rescues a product that is not superior.** "marketing never makes up for a product that isn't superior" (S007) "you can have the best marketing in the world people will buy your product once if it's not great" (S007) Sources: 9 (S007, S010, S018, S022, S023, S033, S038, S050, S057). Confidence: high.

18. **Watch the customer and your own invention, not the competition.** "i have not focused on the competition i find that when you start to really pay attention to the competition that's when things are probably not they're probably going to start heading south for you" (S020) "I'm very laser on the customer and my why." (S014) Sources: 8 (S009, S014, S020, S022, S032, S037, S038, S044). Confidence: high.

19. **Name and package for the shelf, because with no ad budget the shelf does the selling.** "Like a name is so important. It's such a part of the equation of the formula." (S035) "it was so unheard of in 2000 to put don't worry we've got your butt covered on a packaging that was at nean Marcus like that kind of marketing speak did not exist" (S006) Sources: 8 (S002, S006, S013, S016, S018, S023, S035, S038). Confidence: high.

20. **Keep a new idea private, except with people who can build it, until no one could talk you out of it.** "I didn't want to tell friends and family, because I didn't want to invite ego into the process too soon, so I kept it a secret from everybody in my life, and didn't seek validation." (S008) "I feel that ideas are the most vulnerable in the moment that you have them." (S053) Sources: 7 (S001, S008, S009, S015, S016, S045, S053). Confidence: high. Her husband argues for going public early; she grants both can work (S053).

21. **There is no wrong time to start if the product is really better; do not wait to feel ready.** "If you have truly invented something or figured out a way to make something better, there's no there's no wrong time to start a company." (S014) "if you wait for the right time it never comes amen here's your news it never comes so you have to jump" (S040) Sources: 7 (S010, S014, S032, S037, S040, S042, S050). Confidence: high.

22. **Give every person in the chain their what's-in-it-for-them, fast.** "you always want to give them a what's in it for them." (S004) "very focused on what's in it for them that's kind of all my mind is thinking about when I'm talking to somebody what's in it for them" (S040) Sources: 6 (S004, S007, S009, S040, S041, S050). 23. **Raising money is dilution, not an achievement; do not let ego push you to start big.** "you are diluting yourself. So why are we all high fiving you?" (S045) "I think a lot of entrepreneurs make the mistake of not starting small" (S037) Sources: 7 (S014, S016, S019, S032, S037, S040, S045). Confidence: high. 24. **Do not let investors take away your ability to follow your intuition.** "if I had investors, I knew that I wouldn't be able to honor my intuition." 25. **Business is not war; win by filling a void and treating customers kindly.** "I don't think business is war. I don't think it has to be that way. 26. **Celebrate mistakes in public and never let one cost someone their job.** "At Spanx I really encourage people to fail. Um we had something in our culture called oops meetings." 27. **Keep the day job until the idea has real orders.** "I didn't want to quit my job and have no income and no healthare" (S026) Sources: 2 (S001, S026). Confidence: medium. Her line elsewhere: entrepreneurs mitigate risk rather than take it (S001).

28. **In a crisis, over-communicate, cut from a zero-based budget, and keep moving.** "in crisis I say keep going forward" (S042) "during crisis is a good time to actually build culture because people will remember how you showed up for them in big and in small ways." 29. **Growth for its own sake is not the goal; grow as fast as you can serve her.** "growth for the sake of growth was not ever anything that I aimed for" (S040) "i was always so focused on the customer and so my my business grew as I could serve her" (S042) Sources: 3 (S019, S040, S042).
