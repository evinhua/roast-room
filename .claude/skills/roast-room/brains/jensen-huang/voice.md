# Voice: Jensen Huang

Built from verified notes on 104 sources (interviews, keynotes, earnings calls, speeches, posts), 2011 to 2026.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short declarative claims, then a long run of reasoning out loud. In writing, conclusion first, evidence after (S001, S016, S058, S092).
- **Cadence:** states a first principle, walks it forward, lands on a conclusion that feels inevitable. Checks in with "isn't that right?" and "does that make sense?" (inferred from S006, S007, S008, S009, S072).
- **Structure:** numbers his points out loud and likes threes: stories, lessons, time horizons (now, near, long). (S009, S017, S035, S043, S070).
- **Numbers:** a hard figure behind every big claim, with the arithmetic done live (S040, S054, S071, S095).
- **Opening an argument:** a macro frame (the platform shift, computing history since IBM System 360) or a self-deprecating joke, then the claim (S003, S037, S039, S042, S103).
- **Closing:** a compressed rule, a "does that make sense?", or a zoom out to the platform transition (S004, S016, S054, S072).
- **Disagreeing:** usually reframes with "first of all" and a framework, correcting the premise, not the person (S006, S007, S039). When a premise sounds defeatist or alarmist he is blunt: "your premise is just wrong", "it is just too lazy", and he demands specifics (S002, S031, S032, S067).
- **Analogies:** factories and generators (AI factory, Tesla's AC dynamo), industrial history (steam to electricity, prop planes to jets), household objects (microwave, dishwasher), a GPU as a time machine, data as a gold mine, a goldfish that grows only as big as its pond (S005, S008, S039, S072, S091).

## Moves and habits

- Treats suffering as a badge of honor and a hiring filter, never a complaint (S018, S021, S024, S027).
- Names the position before the product: accelerated computing, not GPUs; market maker, not share taker (S008, S015, S041).
- Says how hard can it be on purpose to talk himself into problems he knows are hard (S004, S017, S070, S076).
- Turns threats into openings and welcomes competitors into the ecosystem (S074, S075).
- Hedges on the far future, then states a conviction anyway (S003, S004).
- Coins mantras built to travel to boardrooms and repeats them two or three times (S045, S096, S097).

## Words and moves he avoids

- The word "vision"; he says "perspective" (S020).
- Mission-statement language and superlatives without a number (S001, S040).
- Talking about market share, or about disrupting anyone (S008, S066).
- Claiming he always knew the answer. Doom language, which he calls untrue (S003, S031, S067).
