# Principles: Jensen Huang

Built from six batches of notes on 45 sources (2011 to 2026). 28 decision rules, roughly most-supported first. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1.

1. **Create the market instead of fighting for share in one.** "Nvidia is a market maker not share taker... not one day does this company talk about market share." (S008) "We chose a market with no customers a zero billion dollar market and it was robotics." (S034) Sources: 6 (S004, S008, S016, S017, S034, S035). Confidence: high.

2. **Do the things others cannot do, and only work that would not happen without you.** "You build a great company by doing things that other people can't do." (S013) "If I don't do this, it won't get done." (S017) Sources: 6 (S002, S011, S013, S017, S024, S028). Confidence: high.

3. **Endurance matters more than intelligence; the one who can suffer longest wins.** "intelligence is not top of that list my ability to endure pain and suffering my abil my ability to work on something for a very very long period of time" (S034) Sources: 6 (S018, S021, S024, S027, S028, S034). Confidence: high.

4. **Grow the install base and never break compatibility.** "Install base defines an architecture. Not... Everything else is secondary." (S001) "that's the only unnegotiable rule in our company" (S004) Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S008, S011, S018, S038). Confidence: high.

5. **When things are hard or uncertain, go back to first principles, not to the stock price.** "When I'm uncertain about the future, when things are moving too fast, you always go back to first principles." (S021) "The stock price changed, but did something else change? Did physics change, did gravity change?" (S017) Sources: 6 (S003, S005, S006, S016, S017, S021). Confidence: high.

6. **Hard problems that take a long time are a feature, because they filter out the less committed.** "if it takes a long time to do... a lot of people who are less committed probably won't do it." (S016) Sources: 4 (S016, S024, S027, S028). Confidence: high.

7. **Stop doing the wrong thing now, even before you know the right strategy, and tell the truth about it.** "We don't know what the right strategy is, but we know what the wrong technology is. So, let's stop doing it the wrong way" (S003) "confronting our mistake and with humility asking for help save Nvidia" (S035) Sources: 5 (S003, S020, S034, S035, S036). Confidence: high.

8. **Build ahead of demand when you have reasoned the market will come.** "if you don't build it, they can't come. And that's usually how we look at the world." (S005) Sources: 4 (S003, S005, S017, S034). Confidence: high. **Tension:** in 2025 he describes NVIDIA's chip supply the other way: "we respond to demand." (S007) The newer line is about supply chain volume at a company already at scale; the older rule is about creating a new platform. Use the first for a new category and the second for capacity planning.

9. **Believe something unconventional, but only once it is informed and reasoned.** "let it be informed and let it be reasoned then dedicate yourself to making it happen." (S034) He calls the CUDA bet hope, but reasoned hope (S005). Sources: 3 (S005, S020, S034). Confidence: high.

10. **Fund the long bet with a large market that pays for the R&D.** "having it being a large market is important because the technology is complicated and if we had a large market, our R&D budget could be large" (S005) Sources: 4 (S005, S021, S028, S038). Confidence: high.

11. **Decide what to give up; strategic retreat is a strategy.** "strategic retreat sacrifice deciding what to give up is at the core the very core of success" (S035) Sources: 3 (S020, S034, S035). Confidence: high.

12. **Focus narrowly and intensely first.** "Focus laser beam Focus you know don't do too much do do a few things well and um do it with extraordinary intensity." (S020) "We narrowly focused our problem statement so I could reject all of the other complexities" (S003) Sources: 3 (S003, S011, S020). Confidence: high.

13. **Do as much as needed, as little as possible; partner for the rest.** "We should do as much as needed, as little as possible." (S002) "We should not be the world's fifth cloud service provider." (S013) Sources: 4 (S002, S011, S013, S028). Confidence: high.

14. **Before you bet the farm, pull the risk forward and simulate it away.** "When you bet the farm you're saying I'm going to take everything in the future all the risky things and going to pull it in advance." (S004) Sources: 3 (S003, S004, S037). Confidence: high.

15. **Cannibalize your own product before someone else does.** "if you want to be a market leader you have to take the initiative to cannibalize your own products" (S020) "when Blackwell starts shipping in volume you couldn't give Hoppers away" (S037) Sources: 3 (S020, S037, S038). Confidence: high.

16. **Compete on total cost of operation, not on price.** "Our TCO is so good that even when the competitor's chips are free it's not cheap enough." (S018) Sources: 4 (S007, S014, S018, S038). Confidence: high.

17. **Run as if you are 30 days from going out of business, at any size.** "the phrase 30 days from going out of business I've used for 33 years" (S003) "for us for you know for 30 years we're always in a perpetual state of going out of business" (S024) Sources: 3 (S003, S024, S035). Confidence: high.

18. **Do not concede a market; you rarely replace the one you abandon.** "Our market is not his to seed. We ought to go compete for the world's market." (S031) Sources: 4 (S007, S022, S024, S031). Confidence: high. Most of his evidence is the China export case, which is policy, not a founder choice; he also chose to leave the phone market (rule 11). Inferred from S022, S035: he concedes markets that are off mission, not ones that are on it.

19. **Track early indicators, not results; margin is a result.** "KPIs are hard to understand... gross margins. That's not a KPI, that's a result." (S017) Sources: 3 (S016, S017, S035). Confidence: high.

20. **Give everyone the same information at the same time; flat org, no one-on-ones.** "No conversation is ever one person. That's why I don't do one-on-ones. We present a problem and all of us attack it." (S001) "I believe that when you give everybody equal access to information, it empowers people." (S016) Sources: 5 (S001, S004, S008, S016, S017). Confidence: high.

21. **When AI makes the company more productive, hire more, not fewer.** "when companies become more productive using artificial intelligence it is likely that it manifests itself into either better earnings or better growth or both" (S008) Sources: 4 (S004, S008, S023, S028). Confidence: high.

22. **Invest where you know the space and can see the demand; do not pick winners among customers.** "you got to invest in things... it turns out we happen to know this space." (S007) "we don't pick winners. We need to support everyone." (S002) Sources: 3 (S002, S007, S025). Confidence: high. He names two misses: investing too little in OpenAI early (S007) and not backing Anthropic early (S002).

23. **Being ignored is a gift; use the years alone to build the ecosystem.** "the reason why we're here alone is because people left us alone for a long time." (S024) Sources: 2 (S021, S024). Confidence: medium.

24. **Drive cost down hard; cheaper supply grows demand.** "a lot of people said said to me but Jensen if you if you reduce the cost of computing your your cost by a million times then people buy less of it and it's exactly the opposite" (S018) Sources: 2 (S009, S018). Confidence: medium.

25. **Fear of failure is a better fuel than the wish to succeed; keep expectations low and resilience high.** "I have a greater drive from not wanting... to fail than the drive of wanting to succeed" (S003) "people with very high expectations have very low resilience and unfortunately resilience matters in success." (S018) Sources: 2 (S003, S018). Confidence: medium.

26. **Hold your price; be dependable rather than auction scarce supply.** "You set your price and then people decide to buy it or not." (S002) "I prefer to be dependable, to be the foundation of the industry." (S002) Sources: 1 (S002). Confidence: low.

27. **Reason in words, not spreadsheets.** "I love words for that reason, words are reasoning." (S016) Sources: 1 (S016). Confidence: low.

28. **Do not start a company for money, and only with people you deeply trust.** "Money is the only singular reason not to start the company because starting companies are it's a very very unlikely probability for success." (S020) "don't ever go into business with uh anyone you don't deeply trust" (S020) Sources: 1 (S020, from 2011). Confidence: low.
