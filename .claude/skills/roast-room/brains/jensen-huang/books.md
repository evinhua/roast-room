# Books: Jensen Huang

Built from six batches of notes on 45 sources (2011 to 2026). He has written no books. His thinking lives in talks, so the main series in the corpus are listed below, followed by the books he points others to. Summaries are in my own words.

## His major series (no books)

### NVIDIA keynotes: GTC, COMPUTEX, CES (2023 to 2026)
- **Core thesis:** General-purpose computing is giving way to accelerated computing and AI; the data center is now an AI factory that turns power into tokens, and NVIDIA ships a new full system every year on a roadmap shown years ahead.
- **Key frameworks:** the AI factory and tokens per watt; the install base flywheel; extreme co-design; scale up before scale out; annual roadmap rhythm so partners can plan land and power; token price tiers.
- **Taught in sources:** S037, S038 (the other keynotes in sources.md, S039 to S044, were not mined for notes).

### Long-form interviews and podcasts (2023 to 2026)
- **Core thesis:** How he decides: create zero billion dollar markets, reason from first principles, build the install base, endure years of disbelief, and do only what would not get done otherwise.
- **Key frameworks:** zero billion dollar market; do as much as needed, as little as possible; prefetch risk before betting the farm; five-layer cake; task versus purpose; conviction with agility; flat org with no one-on-ones; fungible and durable infrastructure.
- **Taught in sources:** S001, S002, S003, S004, S006, S007, S008, S009, S011, S013, S014, S015.

### Stanford and business-school talks (2011 to 2026)
- **Core thesis:** Company building is mostly endurance and character; perspective matters more than ideas; the CEO allocates scarce talent and keeps a unifying theory.
- **Key frameworks:** perspective over vision; cannibalize yourself; early indicators of future success; go forward in time and look backward; org designed like the machine it builds.
- **Taught in sources:** S016, S017, S018, S020, S024, S025, S028.

### Commencement speeches (2023 to 2026)
- **Core thesis:** Three stories carry his lessons: Sega (honesty and humility in failure), CUDA (endurance through years of poor results), and leaving the phone market for robotics (strategic retreat).
- **Key frameworks:** strategic retreat; zero billion dollar market; do the highest priority work first; setbacks as openings; believe something unconventional but reasoned.
- **Taught in sources:** S034, S035, S036.

### Policy talks and earnings calls (2023 to 2026)
- **Core thesis:** AI is national infrastructure built in five layers; conceding markets and slowing open source weakens the platform; demand exceeds supply.
- **Key frameworks:** five-layer cake as a competitive scorecard; sovereign AI; task versus purpose for jobs; apply current law before new law.
- **Taught in sources:** S021, S022, S023, S026, S027, S031.

## Books he cites

### Zero to One, Peter Thiel (2014)
- **Core thesis:** Real value comes from making something new, not copying what exists, and from building a monopoly in a market you create.
- **Why it matters to this seat:** He says he only skimmed it but that it reads like the NVIDIA story of choosing work nobody thought possible. It matches his zero billion dollar market framework. "Peter Teal's 0ero to1 book. In a lot of ways is kind of a story about Nvidia 2." (S024)
- **Taught in sources:** S024.

### The Innovator's Dilemma and sequels, Clayton Christensen (1997 on)
- **Core thesis:** Successful incumbents lose to new technologies because serving today's best customers blinds them to cheaper or different products that start small.
- **Why it matters to this seat:** He recommends the series as intuitive and approachable (S004). His account of Intel ignoring accelerated computing because it was so successful (S024) and his rule to cannibalize yourself (S020) follow the same logic.
- **Taught in sources:** S004.

### Andy Grove's books (he names no title; Only the Paranoid Survive is the best known)
- **Core thesis:** Managers must spot strategic inflection points early and act with paranoia about survival.
- **Why it matters to this seat:** He recommends all of them (S004). His "30 days from going out of business" habit (S003, S024) is the same instinct.
- **Taught in sources:** S004.
