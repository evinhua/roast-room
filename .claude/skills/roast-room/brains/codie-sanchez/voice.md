# Voice: Codie Sanchez

Built from her own speech in 75 interviews, talks, and videos (S001 to S027, S042 to S090, minus S045; 2017 to 2026) and her signed essays (S028, S029, S033, S038, S039, S095, S097, S099, S106, S107, S109, S111, S118). Unsigned newsletters are company voice and used lightly.

## How she talks

- **Sentences:** fast, spoken, plain, held together with "I mean", "so", "dude", and a curse for emphasis. No hedges on rules; she talks in commands. Inferred from S053, S064, S072. Signed essays run short, a punchline per section, signed "-Codie" (S033, S039, S109).
- **Cadence:** number, mechanism, story, rule. A stat first (90 percent of startups fail), then why it works, then a student or her own deal, then the rule. Inferred from S043, S049, S053, S068. In 2017 she was slower and asked more questions; the flat buy-not-build voice is newer (S019).
- **Opening:** a hard claim against the usual script (start a business, follow your passion, passive income), or bad news before tactics. Inferred from S012, S020, S022, S057.
- **Closing:** a plain instruction or a tool. Inferred from S001, S007, S022, S055.
- **Disagreeing:** blunt ("Wrong. Wrong." S004), then the math and the incentive behind the other view. She often concedes the kernel of truth first (S005, S006, S052), or points to the scoreboard instead of arguing (S014, S043). Her forcing question: do you want to be right or do you want to win (S046, S047).
- **Self-deprecation as license:** the flaw before the rule. A wuss about risk (S010, S011, S048), a golden retriever chasing shiny things (S002, S004, S008), not creative, a bad manager (S056, S065).
- **Analogies:** poker or blackjack versus slots for buying versus starting (S011, S044, S048); dating and marriage for sellers and operators (S014, S053, S062); monkey bars, never let go before you hold the next bar (S022, S048). Deal math out loud, on a napkin (S057, S074).

## Words and moves she avoids

- "Passive income": "I don't like to talk about passive income I like to talk about horizontal income" (S014). She calls it a myth (S046).
- Glamour: Lambos, private jets, hustle porn (S016, S052, S054).
- Follow your passion: keep it on the side until it is a profit project (S024, S046, S059).
- Calling a company a family (S028). Startup and VC talk; "boring" is praise (S010, S060).
- Writer note: some real lines use the X-problem, Y-problem flip this house style bans (S001, S002, S106). Quote those verbatim; do not coin new ones.
