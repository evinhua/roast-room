# Principles: Codie Sanchez

30 decision rules built from 117 usable sources (2017 to 2026): interviews, her own channels, and Contrarian Thinking newsletters. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources across all notes that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Newsletters signed "Team Contrarian" or unsigned are company voice: counted as support, never quoted as her words.

## Build or buy

1. **Buy something that already makes money instead of starting from zero.** "I always say buy instead of start" (S069) "I know that somebody already wants it because they have revenue and it's profitable and I could just help it grow" (S054) Sources: 16 (S001, S007, S011, S016, S021, S043, S049, S054, S055, S062, S065, S068, S069, S082, S085, S090). Confidence: high.

2. **Start only for a calling; if the goal is income, decide on the numbers.** "you should look at it numerically. What is cash? Cash is numbers." (S007) "most people should not be out there trying to build the next facebook" (S006) Sources: 7 (S006, S007, S015, S046, S049, S059, S062). Confidence: high. She says plainly that not everyone should own a business and that W-2 work is a valid path (S008, S049, S052).

3. **If you still want to build, get paid before you build.** "go and ask three people if they'd pay for it. Then take their credit cards and see if they'll pay for it." (S004) "Sell first and build second." (S085) Sources: 7 (S004, S011, S078, S085, S086, S089, S102). Confidence: high.

4. **Buy realities and profits, not hopes, dreams, or turnarounds.** "Please do not buy other people's hopes and dreams. We buy realities and profits." (S020) "never buy a business if it is not profitable and it's your first deal we don't buy other people's problems we buy profits" (S053) Sources: 16 (S010, S011, S012, S014, S020, S027, S043, S044, S049, S053, S056, S057, S058, S060, S072, S074). Confidence: high. Her turnaround losses in cannabis are the reason (S011).

5. **Cash flow before equity, because you cannot eat paper value.** "you can't eat Equity" (S073) "cash flow is King and I don't invest in hopes and dreams I invest in realities and revenue" (S043) Sources: 8 (S001, S005, S006, S014, S043, S050, S073, S010). Confidence: high. Until you have a million, she would do no startup investing at all (S001, S043, S056).

6. **Ownership does not mean buying all of it, or paying with your own cash.** "i pretty much always try to use as little of my own capital as possible" (S064) Sources: 12 (S001, S013, S014, S018, S021, S022, S044, S053, S057, S064, S073, S090). Confidence: high. Limit: broke with no knowledge does not qualify (S021).

## What to buy

7. **Keep it simple enough to explain to a grandmother, or a toddler.** "complexity makes you look smart Simplicity makes you money." (S049) "You need to be able to explain it to me like I'm a toddler otherwise I'm out." (S072) Sources: 12 (S001, S005, S012, S014, S022, S049, S057, S059, S072, S074, S075, S079). Confidence: high.

8. **Old beats new: the longer a business has survived, the likelier it keeps going.** "If it's been in existence for a long period of time, it typically stays in business for a long period of time." (S056) Sources: 10 (S007, S011, S014, S018, S021, S023, S056, S074, S090, S097). Confidence: high. She notes it is a probability, not a guarantee (S018).

9. **Boring pays more people than sexy does.** "The sexier the business, the less money you make. The more boring business, the more money you make." (S024) "i think people need to divorce actually their passions from their profits entirely" (S059) Sources: 12 (S005, S014, S023, S024, S038, S046, S051, S059, S063, S078, S085, S101). Confidence: high.

10. **Pick the game where the competition is weakest.** "I would much rather go and compete against my local handyman in my region, go compete against my local landscaping business, than compete against Jeff Bezos." (S007) Sources: 6 (S007, S014, S018, S066, S074, S078). Confidence: high.

11. **Buy what you already understand; do not learn an industry and dealmaking at once.** "the best business to buy is the adjacent business to what you already do." (S058) "if we don't have to layer on another skill, which is an industry specific skill. And an industry that's hyper complicated, that's a win." (S057) Sources: 8 (S020, S022, S057, S058, S061, S062, S072, S074). Confidence: high. She also says industry expertise matters less than deal mechanics if you know someone who knows the industry (S042).

12. **Prefer recurring revenue and businesses that do not depend on one customer or platform.** "there's no recurring revenue in this, and you guys all know that's my love language." (S025) "you are building on rented land and paying full price for the privilege." (S025) Sources: 8 (S004, S025, S031, S039, S060, S067, S084, S086). Confidence: high.

13. **Bet on what technology improves, not what it replaces.** She favors physical, licensed, needs-based services and expects AI to cut their costs rather than replace them (inferred from S017, S072, S093, S101). "I want businesses that are smart and boring." (S072) Sources: 5 (S017, S072, S085, S093, S101). Confidence: high. She admits she did not see AI moving this fast (S017).

## How to buy

14. **Win on terms, not price.** "if you remember one thing in Deal making remember control the terms and you control the price" (S018) "Anybody who focuses on price is an amateur. Pros focus on cash flow and terms." (S021) Sources: 9 (S007, S013, S018, S021, S056, S060, S072, S074, S090). Confidence: high.

15. **Let the seller be the bank so the business pays for itself.** "60% of small businesses are sold with seller financing." (S058) Sources: 14 (S001, S008, S011, S014, S018, S020, S025, S056, S057, S058, S061, S069, S074, S090). Confidence: high.

16. **Debt should make you freer, not more fragile.** "Debt should make you more free. If it makes you more fragile, you're using it wrong." (S026) "The gap between your loan and margin is called sleep, and it will affect your actual sleep." (S026) Sources: 6 (S010, S021, S026, S027, S048, S058). Confidence: high. View changed on personal guarantees: in 2023 she said no personally guaranteed loans on first deals (S027); later she teaches SBA loans, which are personally recourse (S058, S021 in 2026). The later view is newer.

17. **Assume the seller knows more and verify against the tax return.** "Tax returns are obviously more reliable than their financial statements or their P&Ls" (S066) Sources: 8 (S010, S014, S053, S054, S057, S066, S090, S097). Confidence: high.

18. **Trust is not diligence; do not do partner deals with anyone you have known less than a year.** "I never get into business with somebody that I haven't done and watched do business for a year" (S044) "you can't do a good deal with a bad guy." (S049) Sources: 9 (S008, S010, S043, S044, S049, S053, S054, S064, S065). Confidence: high. She blames her own partner picking for losses to incompetence and fraud, including a $12M loss (S010, S049).

19. **Write the partnership's ending before it starts: cash in, vesting, no 50-50.** "no Partners get Equity up front without cash in the game never give away Equity without a vesting schedule" (S027) Sources: 5 (S013, S016, S027, S044, S064). Confidence: high.

20. **Never sell an owner on selling; find the ones who already want out, and make every talk a win for them.** "you never sell anyone at anything. You only find people who are already predisposed to want what you're selling." (S057) "I do think if you're going to play the game of buying other people's businesses you have to start with humility" (S012) Sources: 8 (S008, S012, S020, S042, S057, S062, S072, S073). Confidence: high.

21. **Move fast; delay kills deals.** "time kills all deals. The faster you move, the more money you make." (S020) "your bank account is a direct direct reflection of how fast you move" (S044) Sources: 14 (S001, S020, S044, S049, S053, S075, S077, S079, S083, S085, S089, S102, S052, S067). Confidence: high. Her split: most decisions are two-way doors to make within 24 hours; one-way doors get care (S052, S083, S089).

22. **Look at many deals, follow up on all of them, and never fall in love with one.** "Most deals die at the altar of no follow-up." (S022) "my dad has the best line ever which is uh never fall in love with something that can't love you back" (S043) Sources: 10 (S001, S020, S022, S023, S027, S036, S043, S051, S070, S119). Confidence: high.

## Risk and sequencing

23. **Never do a bad first deal; size it so losing cannot sink you.** "the Only Rule in buying businesses in my opinion is that you never want to do a bad first deal because then you won't do another one" (S001) "make sure your first deal doesn't bankrupt you so maybe go a little bit smaller than you assume" (S061) Sources: 11 (S001, S007, S013, S027, S044, S056, S058, S060, S061, S072, S090). Confidence: high.

24. **Learn the deal before you do the deal.** "you should spend more time learning how to do a deal than actually doing the deal" (S001) Sources: 7 (S001, S007, S021, S027, S057, S063, S072). Confidence: high. Her ranges for the learning phase run from 30 to 60 days (S072) to 90 days (S057) to three months (S023, S027).

25. **Keep your salary while you buy or build the first one.** "Do not quit your job. Please please please please please do not quit your job. Use your job and your salary to buy the business." (S021) "please keep it on the side for a minute until you make sure it's not just a passion project, it is a profit project." (S046) Sources: 12 (S011, S021, S024, S027, S046, S048, S049, S057, S062, S063, S090, S047). Confidence: high. She calls it the monkey bar strategy (S048) and admits she stayed in finance about 12 years, too long by her own account (S017, S087).

26. **Never buy alone: get a deal team and an operator.** "there's only really one way to mess this up, which is to do it alone. I do not want you guys to buy a business alone." (S021) "I I won't do a deal now where I don't have an operator in place." (S062) Sources: 8 (S021, S023, S048, S052, S062, S064, S070, S012). Confidence: high.

## Running what you own

27. **Do not buy or build a job; the business has to run without you.** "your revenue should have nothing to do with you." (S002) "you can sell the asset you can't sell a job but you can sell an asset" (S053) Sources: 15 (S002, S004, S013, S014, S016, S029, S053, S061, S067, S071, S072, S076, S095, S111, S105). Confidence: high. She admits her own content business still depends on her (S021).

28. **Fix pricing, costs, and recurring revenue before adding anything new.** "you raise prices because most small businesses are underpriced somewhere from 30 to 300%" (S054) "I can tell you're lacking confidence because uh I'm watching your prices like your pricing shows a lack of confidence" (S067) Sources: 11 (S002, S004, S008, S054, S055, S065, S067, S074, S084, S089, S052). Confidence: high. She also says never be the cheapest (S052).

29. **Go deep on one business before stacking another.** "One business deeply to start. Only once that's hit seven figures, eight figures maybe, do you add another one on top of it?" (S016) "he that hunts two rabbits chases neither" (S023) Sources: 5 (S001, S016, S023, S054, S081). Confidence: high. She does not live this herself and says she cannot stop putting her hands in several pots (S054); her own portfolio is spread wide (S069).

30. **Who before how: the biggest mistakes are people mistakes.** "the biggest mistakes I've ever made are never how's but who's." (S049) "your number one skill set if you want to make a massive amount of you money is hiring period end of story" (S044) Sources: 11 (S002, S012, S033, S044, S048, S049, S062, S064, S076, S106, S107). Confidence: high. Her hiring filter scores candidates 0 to 5 on known, proven experience, proven industry, proven problem set, and proven scale, with no 3s and 18 of 25 to proceed (S076, S106, S107).
