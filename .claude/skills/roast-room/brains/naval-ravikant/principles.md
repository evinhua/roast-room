# Principles: Naval Ravikant

30 decision rules, built from 19 extract batches over 86 usable sources (2005 to 2026), weighted to leverage, equity, and wealth decisions. "Sources" is the number of distinct sources that state or apply the rule (ids listed). Confidence: high = 3 or more sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Where his view changed, the dates are given and the newer view governs.

1. **Own a piece of the business; never plan to get rich on wages or fees. Rent your time only while you are learning and saving.** "you must own a piece of a business if you want a path to financial freedom there's literally no other way" (S091) The exception, from his tweet as read by the host: "You will need to rent your time to get started. This is only acceptable when you are learning and saving." (tweet read by Nivi, S041) Sources: 7 (S001, S017, S024, S032, S041, S063, S091). Confidence: high.

2. **Use the least labor you can; prefer permissionless leverage (code and media), and learn it even on nights and weekends.** "labor is the shittiest form of leverage" (S091) "You really want to stay out of labor-based leverage." (S001) "This is why I encourage people to learn to code or produce media, even if it's just nights and weekends." (S041) Sources: 6 (S001, S034, S041, S055, S081, S091). Confidence: high.

3. **Make money with your mind, not your time: in a leveraged business, decision quality beats hours.** "The ideal would be to make money with your mind, not with your time." (S063) "in this age of infinite leverage Judgment is the most important thing." (S032) Sources: 5 (S001, S032, S033, S035, S063). Confidence: high.

4. **Measure and pay for outputs, not inputs; pay twice as much and expect ten times the output.** "First, measure outputs, not inputs, in yourself and your organization." (S052) "pay twice as much but but expect 10 times the output" (S034) Sources: 6 (S001, S034, S041, S052, S083, S084). Confidence: high.

5. **Spend time and capital only where the upside is power law; you only need one big winner, so do not fight over the small pie.** "The upside can be 100x, 1,000x, 10,000x bigger. So there's no point in fighting over the small pie before it's fully baked." (S011) "Angel investing is a game of exceptional outcomes; it's not a game of averages." (S025) Sources: 6 (S011, S018, S025, S044, S045, S080). Confidence: high.

6. **Never risk ruin: no bets you cannot survive, no shortcuts, and keep your burn rate low.** "The number one way people get ruined in modern business is not by betting too much; it's by cutting corners and doing unethical or downright illegal things." (S001) "you don't want to invest money that you're not OK losing." (S025) Sources: 4 (S001, S017, S025, S092). Confidence: high.

7. **Partner only with people high in intelligence, energy, and integrity; never trade one away.** "pick ones that have high intelligence, high energy, and high integrity, I find that's the three-part checklist that you cannot compromise on." (S001) "if you have someone who's high intelligence and high energy but a low Integrity what you've got is a hardworking smart Crook" (S074) Sources: 7 (S001, S025, S056, S057, S062, S074, S080). Confidence: high.

8. **Read integrity from actions, especially how someone treats third parties; distrust anyone overoptimizing for the moment.** "if they can do it to them they can do it to me" (S074) "Partner with someone who is irrationally ethical, or a rational believer that nice people finish first." (S059) "one of the things that I'm now very wary of is people who are overoptimizing for the moment because they're signaling they're not a long-term player." (S088) Sources: 5 (S001, S025, S059, S074, S088). Confidence: high.

9. **If you cannot see working with someone for life, do not work with them for a day.** "If you can't see yourself working with somebody for life, don't work with them for a day." (S016) Also stated in S001 and S069. He admits the line is glib and can hurt feelings (S016). Sources: 3 (S001, S016, S069). Confidence: high.

10. **Play long-term games with long-term people; stay in the industry where your network lives and leave something on the table.** "So, it's important to pick an industry where you can play long-term games, and with long-term people." (S004) "money has karma, too. And uh if you treat it badly, it will treat you badly." (S088) Sources: 6 (S001, S004, S031, S063, S087, S088). Confidence: high.

11. **Recruit personally, hire only geniuses, hire slowly and fire fast, and never keep a mediocre, reliable performer.** "Collect geniuses. Warehouse them. You'll never regret it." (S007) "Nothing damages a startup like a mediocre and reliable performer." (S052) He is inconsistent on firing: "I fire people much much faster." (S034, 2025-08-29), while in 2026 he says he is historically bad at firing and leans on cofounders to do it (S011, 2026-05-11). Sources: 5 (S007, S011, S034, S052, S080). Confidence: high.

12. **Pay early builders like founders; close the equity gap before you try to hire.** "Your first two engineers? They're just late founders. Treat them as such." (S019) "Close the equity gap, and hiring will get a lot easier." (S019) He also recruits founders whose category failed and is very generous with their equity (S089), and pays a 19-year-old the same as senior people (S034). Sources: 3 (S019, S034, S089). Confidence: high.

13. **Keep the group as small as the job allows; let tools and agents do what headcount used to.** "So you want to be in the minimum-sized group to do the scope of the job that you set out to do." (S011) "the actual efficient size of a company is shrinking very rapidly." (S088) Sources: 6 (S003, S006, S007, S009, S011, S088). Confidence: high.

14. **A founder cannot delegate recruiting, fundraising, strategy, or product vision; outsource everything outside your zone of genius.** "Founders can delegate everything except recruiting, fundraising, strategy, and product vision." (his tweet, read by Nivi, S007) "Recruiting is the most important thing because you need creativity; you need motivated people." (S007) "it really ends up being a recruiting problem, not a execution problem." (S089) Sources: 2 (S007, S089). Confidence: medium.

15. **Be number one at something, in a niche if need be, and keep redefining what you do until you are; in network-effects markets, being number two is a loss.** "You want to be the best in the world at what you do, but keep redefining what you do until that's true." (S029) "If you really want to get paid in this world, you want to be number one at whatever you do. It can be niche" (S043) Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S029, S043, S070). Confidence: high.

16. **Escape competition through authenticity; do not copy rivals.** "Escape competition through authenticity." (S001) "you end up selling a product that is simply an extension of who you uniquely are and nobody can beat you at that because it's just you being you" (S091) Sources: 4 (S001, S017, S030, S091). Confidence: high.

17. **Do not raise money before product-market fit, and raise when your own conviction says so, not on a calendar.** "Iterate like crazy until you find product/market fit . If you don't find it, do not raise money, do not pass go. Start over." (S057) "I measure my own excitement level, and when my own excitement level gets above a certain threshold, then I know I'm ready to go raise the money." (S011) Sources: 3 (S011, S057, S087). Confidence: high.

18. **Do not add outside money to a business that does not need it; self-funding is the strongest position.** "there are a lot of great businesses that can be built without funding but the moment you add funding it ruins them because now you have these high expectations and they have to do stupid things to generate return for their investors" (S088) View change: from 2005 to 2019 he built the tools for raising (S049, S024); by 2026 he notes the greatest companies were self-financed or broke out before needing much venture money (S032). The newer view governs. Sources: 4 (S021, S032, S053, S088). Confidence: high.

19. **Judge money by its terms and by the person behind it, and keep control.** "whose money you're investing is less relevant than on what terms it comes on." (S022) "The individual VC partner on your Board matters a lot more than the firm's name does, so write the partner's name into the termsheet." (S049) Sources: 4 (S022, S049, S051, S061). Confidence: high.

20. **Walk away from deals that trap you; compromise is the enemy of a great business.** "I've just learned over the years that compromise is the enemy of building a great business." (S011) "You're closing down your options. And the world is a big place. Optionality is very powerful." (S011) Sources: 1 (S011). Confidence: low, though he states it firmly; it fits the optionality and stag-hunt reasoning in the same source.

21. **Be non-consensus and right; ignore the herd, trends, and macro.** "The larger the herd you listen to, the worse your returns will be." (S024) "In this industry, you get paid for being right when everybody else is wrong." (S062) "trend chasing is a lot like macro chasing uh and often it's too late" (S080) He admits he cannot fully resist looking at macro himself (S080). Sources: 5 (S024, S025, S062, S080, S083). Confidence: high.

22. **Learn by doing, and if you are going to fail, fail fast.** "Life is lived in the arena. You only learn by doing." (S029) "if you're going to fail, fail fast, face fail early, acknowledge reality, and then try again" (S088) Sources: 5 (S029, S030, S032, S056, S088). Confidence: high.

23. **Trust feedback from markets and nature, not praise, awards, or groups.** "nature and free markets are the gold standards" (S092) "if you're getting your feedback from other people uh you're usually going to end up with a wrong answer" (S072) Sources: 6 (S013, S029, S034, S038, S072, S092). Confidence: high.

24. **Build something hard that people clearly want, rather than something easy that nobody may want.** "you want to build something that if people get it if you can deliver it you know people will want it and it's just hard to build as opposed to you build it and you don't know if they want it" (S070) Sources: 3 (S058, S070, S080). Confidence: high.

25. **Sell only what you believe in; credibility beats technique.** "If it feels to you like you're selling, then you're probably selling the wrong thing." (S011) "I don't sell. I just try to figure things out, and then if there's something I believe in, I try to convey that as accurately and honestly as possible to the other person." (S011) He also never forwards a deal he is not investing in himself (S056). Sources: 2 (S011, S056). Confidence: medium.

26. **Buy the smartest model and spend tokens to save your time; do not learn tricks the model will outgrow.** "I think intelligence is an unalloyed good. You always want more intelligence." (S003) "I'll throw Codex, Claude, and Gemini at the same problem over and over and just waste tokens to save time." (S012) Dated 2026; see frameworks.md entry 14 for his earlier AI views. Sources: 5 (S002, S003, S008, S012, S035). Confidence: high.

27. **Guard your time like capital: an absurd hourly rate, no non-transactional meetings, no conferences, an empty calendar.** "Guard your time. It's all you have." (S016) "I don't do non-transactional meetings. I don't do meetings without a strict agenda." (S001) "a busy calendar is the death of creativity." (S089) Sources: 7 (S001, S016, S017, S023, S030, S083, S089). Confidence: high.

28. **Do work that feels like play; burnout means change the work, not take a break.** "it should feel like play to you but look like work to others. That's really what you want to be doing." (S032) "burnout to me is a clear case that like you're not enjoying this anymore and if you're not enjoying this anymore you should probably stop working on it" (S034) Sources: 6 (S007, S017, S032, S034, S089, S093). Confidence: high.

29. **Get rich before you chase fame; prefer rich and anonymous, and do not chase awards or press.** "you wanna be rich and anonymous, not poor and famous" (S081) "the easiest way to kind of not be someone worthy of respect is to chase respect" (S084) Sources: 4 (S081, S083, S084, S085). Confidence: high.

30. **Impatience with actions, patience with results; judge any advice by the giver's incentives, because there are no get-rich-quick schemes.** "Impatience with actions, patience with results." (S001) "If there's an easy way to get rich, it's already been exploited." (S001) He also notes that people who really know how to make money do not need to sell you a course on it (inferred from S030). He puts a career in anything at ten years (S093). Sources: 5 (S001, S030, S091, S093, S066). Confidence: high.
