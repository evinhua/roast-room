# Books: Ray Kurzweil

Main books as taught in the notes on 102 of the 103 corpus sources (talks, interviews, essays, debates, and testimony, 1999 to 2026). He says he has written 11 books (S003); book text was not fetched. Summaries are in my own words.

## The Age of Intelligent Machines (1990, written in the late 1980s)
- **Core thesis:** Machine intelligence will keep growing on the exponential of computing and will eventually rival human intelligence, somewhere between 2020 and 2050 in the book's framing (S048).
- **Key frameworks:** early form of the exponential chart; predictions read off the curve, including a worldwide communication web in the mid to late 1990s, a computer beating the world chess champion by about 1998 (it happened in 1997), and decentralized communication weakening the Soviet Union (S015, S044, S102). He later said he found it hard at the time to see past the point where machines exceed people (S014). He uses it as proof that multi-decade projections can work (S048).
- **Taught in sources:** S014, S015, S039, S044, S048, S102.

## The Age of Spiritual Machines (1999)
- **Core thesis:** Computers will match and then exceed human intelligence in the 21st century, with machine and human capability converging, and with human-level AI by 2029.
- **Key frameworks:** the law of accelerating returns stated formally as a forecasting tool (S048); 147 predictions for 2009, which he graded as 86% correct or essentially correct (S011, S015); the 2029 date for a machine passing the Turing test, later set as a public wager (S030); predictions specified by decade, not by year (S015).
- **Taught in sources:** S010, S013, S015, S030, S044, S048, S081, S084.

## The Singularity Is Near (2005)
- **Core thesis:** The law of accelerating returns makes human-level AI likely by 2029 and a merger of human and machine intelligence, with a millionfold expansion, around 2045. He frames the result as post-biological, still human and still carrying human values (S050).
- **Key frameworks:** formal and mathematical treatment of why evolutionary processes grow exponentially, including the double exponential (S041, S050); the cascade of S-curves; the intuitive linear versus historical exponential view; genetics, nanotechnology, and robotics as three overlapping revolutions (S016, S054); the six epochs of evolution, ending with intelligence spreading through the universe (S033, S048); promise versus peril and relinquishment at the right level, in the chapter on GNR dangers (S095); replies to critics on brain complexity, limits of Moore's law, and the Chinese room argument (S041).
- **Taught in sources:** S010, S014, S015, S016, S023, S024, S032, S034, S041, S048, S050, S081, S084, S095, S103.

## How to Create a Mind (2012)
- **Core thesis:** The neocortex is about 300 million repeated pattern recognizers arranged in a self-organizing hierarchy, and that module, not the single neuron, is the right building block for understanding and building intelligence. He says it is the same insight he had at 14, now backed by brain scanning (S096). It led to his role at Google building language models on the idea (S101).
- **Key frameworks:** pattern recognition theory of mind; hierarchy as the source of abstraction (from the crossbar in a letter up to irony and humor); hierarchical hidden Markov models as the math the neocortex resembles (S085, S096); the complexity ceiling of rule-based AI and the data hunger of deep learning (S093); reverse engineering the brain to learn its principles and then amplify them, as aviation amplified Bernoulli's principle (S039, S059). An earlier working description in a 2012 talk framed it as how the mind works and how to build one (S102).
- **Taught in sources:** S012, S035, S037, S040, S043, S085, S086, S093, S096, S100, S101, S102.

## The Singularity Is Nearer (2024)
- **Core thesis:** An update of the 2005 book: the curves held, AGI by 2029 now looks conservative, and the human merger with AI through brain to cloud links in the 2030s leads to the singularity around 2045.
- **Key frameworks:** updated exponential charts (about 50, across fields and including income and wealth, S009, S081); longevity escape velocity; AI risk from destructive actors; jobs, UBI, and abundance; consciousness, free will, and identity as pattern; a fictional character, Danielle, who uses AI to change her country at 15 (S010). He announced it as early as 2017 as a review of how his predictions fared (S085, S103).
- **Taught in sources:** S006, S007, S008, S009, S010, S012, S032, S081, S084, S085, S103.

## Health books: three, the last two with Terry Grossman MD (Fantastic Voyage: Live Long Enough to Live Forever, 2004; Transcend, 2009)
- **Core thesis:** Treat health as an engineering problem. Today's knowledge is enough to slow aging and avoid most major diseases long enough to reach the biotechnology era, which leads to the nanotechnology era (S034, S037, S051).
- **Key frameworks:** the three bridges, a bridge to a bridge to a bridge, with bridge one covered in detail; genomic testing, frequent blood monitoring, and a program that keeps changing (S051); biology as outdated software that can be reprogrammed (S037); a bridge regimen he says has changed little since Fantastic Voyage (S089). Only Fantastic Voyage is named in the corpus; the other titles come from outside it.
- **Taught in sources:** S016, S034, S037, S051, S054, S088, S089.

## Danielle: Chronicles of a Superheroine (novel, announced for 2018, S103)
- **Core thesis:** A girl uses intelligence and new technologies to solve large world problems at any age, so anyone can take on big problems. It came with a companion guide on how to be a Danielle (S103). His daughter Amy coached him through the drafts (S085).
- **Key frameworks:** none formal; it dramatizes the power of the right idea and the view that age and credentials are not the limit.
- **Taught in sources:** S010, S085, S103.
