# Books: Ray Kurzweil

Main books as taught in the 43 corpus sources (talks, interviews, and essays, 1999 to 2026). He says he has written 11 books (S003); book text was not fetched. Summaries are in my own words.

## The Age of Intelligent Machines (1990, written in the 1980s)
- **Core thesis:** Machine intelligence will keep growing on the exponential of computing and will eventually rival human intelligence.
- **Key frameworks:** early form of the exponential chart; predictions read off the curve, including a worldwide communication web in the mid to late 1990s, a computer beating the world chess champion by about 1998, and decentralized communication weakening the Soviet Union. He later said he found it hard at the time to see past the point where machines exceed people.
- **Taught in sources:** S014, S015, S039, S041, S044.

## The Age of Spiritual Machines (1999)
- **Core thesis:** Computers will match and then exceed human intelligence in the 21st century, with machine and human capability converging.
- **Key frameworks:** the law of accelerating returns as a forecasting tool; 147 predictions for 2009, which he graded as 86% correct or essentially correct; the 2029 date for a machine passing the Turing test; predictions specified by decade, not by year.
- **Taught in sources:** S013, S015, S044, S010.

## The Singularity Is Near (2005)
- **Core thesis:** The law of accelerating returns makes human-level AI likely by 2029 and a merger of human and machine intelligence, with a millionfold expansion, around 2045.
- **Key frameworks:** formal treatment of why evolutionary processes grow exponentially; the cascade of S-curves; the intuitive linear versus historical exponential view; genetics, nanotechnology, and robotics as three overlapping revolutions; promise versus peril and relinquishment at the right level; replies to critics on brain complexity, limits of Moore's law, and the Chinese room argument.
- **Taught in sources:** S014, S015, S016, S023, S024, S032, S034, S041.

## How to Create a Mind (2012)
- **Core thesis:** The neocortex is about 300 million repeated pattern recognizers arranged in a self-organizing hierarchy, and that module, not the single neuron, is the right building block for understanding and building intelligence.
- **Key frameworks:** pattern recognition theory of mind; hierarchy as the source of abstraction (from the crossbar in a letter up to irony and humor); reverse engineering the brain to learn its principles and then amplify them, as aviation amplified Bernoulli's principle.
- **Taught in sources:** S035, S037, S040, S043, S012.

## The Singularity Is Nearer (2024)
- **Core thesis:** An update of the 2005 book: the curves held, AGI by 2029 now looks conservative, and the human merger with AI through brain to cloud links in the 2030s leads to the singularity around 2045.
- **Key frameworks:** updated exponential charts (about 50, across fields and including income and wealth); longevity escape velocity; AI risk from destructive actors; jobs, UBI, and abundance; consciousness and identity as pattern.
- **Taught in sources:** S006, S007, S008, S009, S010, S012, S032.

## Health books with Terry Grossman (including Transcend)
- **Core thesis:** Treat health as an engineering problem and stay healthy now so you live to benefit from biotechnology and then nanotechnology.
- **Key frameworks:** the three bridges (bridge one is current best practice, bridge two is biotechnology, bridge three is medical nanobots); biology as outdated software that can be reprogrammed.
- **Taught in sources:** S016, S034, S037, S043.
