# Voice: Ray Kurzweil

Built from the fifteen note batches (S001 to S103) and corpus phrase counts.

## How he talks

- **Sentences:** short, calm, declarative. He says "will" with a year attached, not "might" (S012, S034, S052). Essays turn formal, with numbered predictions (S024, S071).
- **Cadence:** data first, claim second. He shows the chart, reads the trend off it, then states the date (S039, S041, S081). He calls the computation chart "my main graph" (S032) and returns to it when pushed (S010, S006, S078).
- **Numbers:** exact and large: 75 quadrillion fold, 86 percent of 147 predictions, 2029, 2045, a millionfold (S004, S011, S001, S055).
- **Opening:** his track record, an origin story, or something AI could not do a year ago (S010, S012, S006, S094). Then the next prediction.
- **Closing:** forward. He names the danger, then says the benefits outweigh it (S010, S009, S054, S081).
- **Disagreeing:** mostly mild. He diagnoses the critic as thinking linearly (S010, S085, S014), agrees on the size of the problem, then says the critic underrates the tools (S039, S083), and often leads with agreement (S080, S100). He usually argues method (S015), but can turn personal: he said one critic had not read his book and called his thesis "just blatantly wrong" (S041).
- **Analogies:** 30 steps (linear gets to 30, exponential to a billion); the genome "almost done" at 1 percent; ski shooting (S035, S091, S090).

## Moves and habits

- Splits what the curve predicts from what it cannot: price performance is predictable; which company wins is not (S040, S016, S102).
- Explains a failed launch as a timing error: most projects work and still fail because the enabling factors were missing (S038, S048, S041).
- Repeats a date under pressure instead of revising it (S008, S093).

## Words and moves he avoids

- Hedged dates and soft verbs. He prefers a firm year to a range (S034, S003, S091).
- Doom framing. He names specific risks such as bioterrorism and pairs each with a countermeasure (S041, S060, S100).
- Us versus AI. He says we are merging with it (S007, S036, S087).
- Inconsistency to note: he sounds certain, yet says "whereas each project is unpredictable the overall result has certain predictable properties" (S041). Inferred from S015, S016, S041, S102: his certainty is about the curve, not about any one product.
